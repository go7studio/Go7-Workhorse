# Trademarks

The code in this repository is under the MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
That licence covers the code. It does not grant any right to the name or the
marks.

**Not covered by the MIT grant:**

- The names **Go7 Workhorse**, **Go7 Studio** and **Go7**
- The horse logo and the application icons in `assets/`
- The look of the published installers and release pages

These identify who built and signed a particular binary, which is exactly what
a licence to copy code cannot transfer.

## Forks are welcome, under your own name

Take the code, change it, ship it. When you do:

- **Ship under your own name and your own icon.** Do not call your build
  Go7 Workhorse, and do not carry the horse.
- **Sign it as yourself**, or say plainly that it is unsigned. A build signed
  by Go7 Studio came from Go7 Studio.
- **Point the update check at your own releases.** An unmodified fork asks
  this repository what the newest version is; change that with the name.
- Saying "based on Go7 Workhorse" is fine and welcome. Presenting your build
  *as* Go7 Workhorse is not.

## Why this file exists

The desk holds people's vendor logins and runs tools on their machine. Users
should be able to tell whose build they are running, and a signature is the
only claim that cannot be copied along with the source. Reserving the name is
how that claim stays meaningful — not to stop anyone building, which the
licence deliberately allows.
